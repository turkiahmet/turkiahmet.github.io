Turki Al Ahmad

تركي الاحمد

Researcher in Islamic sciences

His research focuses on tafsir, Qur’anic sciences, theology, and religions.

A doctoral student in tafsir at Bayburt University, Türkiye, with a master’s degree in tafsir from Dicle University (2021). His research addresses exegetical literature, methods of Qur’anic interpretation, and the critical editing of manuscripts.

The name is written تركي الاحمد in Arabic, and Türki Ahmet in Turkish.
